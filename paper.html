Video series

Six videos explain the paper, from the question to the proof (playlist; about an hour in total, narrated by a synthetic voice). The last one also describes this Lean companion.

  1. The highest point of a flat interface
  2. The classical recipe and why clusters matter
  3. The tail over one interval
  4. Two separated intervals
  5. The middle range and the blocking argument
  6. What is new, what is open, and the Lean check

A second series of four videos explains the companion paper, Chen and Pu, Two-time spatial decorrelation for the flat KPZ fixed point (arXiv 2607.17113), whose Proposition 3.1 is the hypothesis CubicStrongMixing in the table of inputs (playlist; about 36 minutes in total, narrated by a synthetic voice). Episode 2 explains that mixing bound.

  1. One surface at two times
  2. The Airy₁ process forgets fast
  3. Where the optimizer lives
  4. Spatial averages and the dynamic susceptibility

Authors and AI disclosure

The Lean code is by Le Chen (Auburn University); the mathematics is from the paper by Le Chen and Fei Pu cited above. AI coding assistants, chiefly Anthropic's Claude, helped write the code and the documentation. Lean's kernel checked the proofs; Le Chen is responsible for the statements and their correspondence to the paper.
