Trust and axioms

Generated pages. The verification page and the coverage page are generated by lean-pkg from the recorded receipts, claims.yaml and coverage.yaml of this release. coverage.yaml maps the results of the posted manuscript (arXiv:2611.23658v1) to Lean: 12 of its 24 rows are in Lean with extra hypotheses named on the row, and 12 are outside the table. Theorem 1.1 has three rows, all compared: the statements under three inputs, the two-input form under GOELeftTail and CubicStrongMixing (since v0.9.0), and the one-input form under CubicStrongMixing only (since v0.10.0). That row also names goeLeftTail_holds and airy1FddPositive_holds: the inputs GOELeftTail and Airy1FddPositive are proved with no hypothesis and compared; the original statements still take them as hypotheses, unchanged. Each paper row that takes them also names its input-free _no_fdd_goe_input corollaries. The twelve outside rows are Lemmas 3.3–3.8, the intermediate lemmas of the paper's proof of Proposition 1.4 (proved in the contrib library, 3.6 and 3.7 as stated, 3.3–3.5 in a different form, 3.8 in a weaker form), and Lemmas B.1–B.6 of Appendix B (proved there as stated, B.4 and B.6 on a larger parameter range). They are shown as outside only because coverage rows can name main-package claims alone; each row's note gives the declarations. The Lemma 4.1 row names a fourth input, NonnegativeWindowModulusEstimate (the bound (4.2) on windows inside [0, ∞)), which is assumed, not proved, and which the headline does not use. The Lemma 4.2 row names the association input ProcessAssociated (the association of the Airy₁ process, Pu 2025, Theorem 1.2), which is assumed, not proved, and which the headline does not use. The Proposition 4.3 row names three inputs, each assumed, not proved, and none used by the headline: NonnegativeWindowModulusEstimate, ProcessAssociated and CubicCovarianceDecay (the covariance asymptotic of the Airy₁ process, Basu–Busani–Ferrari 2023, Theorem 1.1). The Remark 5.3 row names the input FerrariLiuPersistence (the finite-length persistence expansion of Ferrari–Liu 2024), which is assumed, not proved, which only Remark 5.3 uses, and which the headline does not use. The comparator compares the seven statements of Challenge.lean (the claims of the three Theorem 1.1 rows); the component results of the nested package contrib/ are listed in contrib/claims.yaml and are not rows of coverage.yaml. A coverage row says how a result is formalized; it is not a human verification of the correspondence (see Trust and axioms).

Trust and axioms

  • No sorry and no new axioms: every audited theorem depends only on propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound.
  • The headline is conditional. Its hypotheses are the three named structures above; read their definitions before relying on the theorem. Lemma 4.1 (block_modulus_airy1) assumes, besides the first two, the fourth named input NonnegativeWindowModulusEstimate, which is not proved here. Lemma 4.2 (tail_sensitive_newman_airy1) assumes, besides the first two, the association input ProcessAssociated (Pu 2025, Theorem 1.2), which is not proved here. Proposition 4.3 (mesoscopic_sum_airy1) assumes, besides the first two, these three: NonnegativeWindowModulusEstimate, ProcessAssociated and the covariance input CubicCovarianceDecay (Basu–Busani–Ferrari 2023, Theorem 1.1), none of which is proved here. Remark 5.3 (gumbel_limit_continuum_airy1_ferrariLiu) assumes, besides the first two, the new input FerrariLiuPersistence (the finite-length persistence expansion of Ferrari–Liu 2024), which is not proved here and which no other result uses.
  • airy1FddPositive_of_goeLeftTail proves Airy1FddPositive under GOELeftTail, and since v0.10.0 goeLeftTail_holds and airy1FddPositive_holds prove both with no hypothesis. The _no_fdd_goe_input headline therefore assumes CubicStrongMixing only, which is not proved here. The original statements still take the records as hypotheses, unchanged. The comparator checks the two _final₂ statements under the three inputs, since v0.9.0 airy1FddPositive_of_goeLeftTail and _final₂_no_fdd_input, and since v0.10.0 goeLeftTail_holds, airy1FddPositive_holds and _final₂_no_fdd_goe_input; the other _no_fdd_input and _no_fdd_goe_input forms are built, not compared.
  • The correspondence between the Lean statements and the paper was checked by AI-assisted review, not by an independent human referee.
