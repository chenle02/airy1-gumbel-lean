Trust and axioms

Generated pages. The verification page and the coverage page are generated by lean-pkg from the recorded receipts, claims.yaml and coverage.yaml of this release. coverage.yaml maps the results of the posted manuscript (arXiv:2611.23658v1) to Lean: 9 of its 10 rows are in Lean with extra hypotheses named on the row, and 1 is outside this formalization (Remark 5.3). The Lemma 4.1 row names a fourth input, NonnegativeWindowModulusEstimate (the bound (4.2) on windows inside [0, ∞)), which is assumed, not proved, and which the headline does not use. The Lemma 4.2 row names the association input ProcessAssociated (the association of the Airy₁ process, Pu 2025, Theorem 1.2), which is assumed, not proved, and which the headline does not use. The Proposition 4.3 row names three inputs, each assumed, not proved, and none used by the headline: NonnegativeWindowModulusEstimate, ProcessAssociated and CubicCovarianceDecay (the covariance asymptotic of the Airy₁ process, Basu–Busani–Ferrari 2023, Theorem 1.1). The comparator compares the two Theorem 1.1 statements of Challenge.lean; the component results of the nested package contrib/ are listed in contrib/claims.yaml and are not rows of coverage.yaml. A coverage row says how a result is formalized; it is not a human verification of the correspondence (see Trust and axioms).

Trust and axioms

  • No sorry and no new axioms: every audited theorem depends only on propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound.
  • The headline is conditional. Its hypotheses are the three named structures above; read their definitions before relying on the theorem. Lemma 4.1 (block_modulus_airy1) assumes, besides the first two, the fourth named input NonnegativeWindowModulusEstimate, which is not proved here. Lemma 4.2 (tail_sensitive_newman_airy1) assumes, besides the first two, the association input ProcessAssociated (Pu 2025, Theorem 1.2), which is not proved here. Proposition 4.3 (mesoscopic_sum_airy1) assumes, besides the first two, these three: NonnegativeWindowModulusEstimate, ProcessAssociated and the covariance input CubicCovarianceDecay (Basu–Busani–Ferrari 2023, Theorem 1.1), none of which is proved here.
  • The correspondence between the Lean statements and the paper was checked by AI-assisted review, not by an independent human referee.
