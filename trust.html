Trust and axioms

Generated pages. The verification page and the coverage page are generated by lean-pkg from the recorded receipts, claims.yaml and coverage.yaml of this release. coverage.yaml maps the results of the posted manuscript (arXiv:2611.23658v1) to Lean: 6 of its 10 rows are in Lean with extra hypotheses named on the row, and 4 are outside this formalization (Lemmas 4.1 and 4.2, Proposition 4.3, Remark 5.3). The comparator compares the two Theorem 1.1 statements of Challenge.lean; the component results of the nested package contrib/ are listed in contrib/claims.yaml and are not rows of coverage.yaml. A coverage row says how a result is formalized; it is not a human verification of the correspondence (see Trust and axioms).

Trust and axioms

  • No sorry and no new axioms: every audited theorem depends only on propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound.
  • The headline is conditional. Its hypotheses are the three named structures above; read their definitions before relying on the theorem.
  • The correspondence between the Lean statements and the paper was checked by AI-assisted review, not by an independent human referee.
