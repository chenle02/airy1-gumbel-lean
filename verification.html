Hypotheses. The paper's, plus these, which the Lean statement assumes and the paper proves or assumes elsewhere: Airy1FddPositive: for every finite set of times, a probability law whose box distribution function is the Airy_1 Fredholm-determinant formula (Quastel-Remenik, Theorem 1; Borodin-Ferrari-Prahofer 2007); GOELeftTail: a cubic left-tail bound for the one-point law (Baik-Buckingham-DiFranco 2008 with the Ferrari-Spohn GOE identity); an input the paper does not need, since it starts from an existing continuous process; CubicStrongMixing: strong mixing between past and future sigma-algebras at rate exp(-c d^3) (Chen-Pu, arXiv 2607.17113, Proposition 3.1).

Axioms. At most Classical.choice, Quot.sound, propext (what the build check records for the whole package).

Note. The process is the version of the Kolmogorov-extension law with continuous paths on [0, infinity); centering and scaling are those of (1.1)-(1.2) for real T. The mesoscopic step is proved by the two-time Fredholm determinant bound and a high-level upcrossing bound, not by association and the covariance asymptotic.
